Kubernetes Relationship Scraper

Go, Kubernetes, Graph Algorithms

A lightweight Go tool that extracts resources from a Kubernetes cluster, builds a real-time graph of their relationships, and outputs it as JSON. It maps connections such as Pod→Node, Pod→ReplicaSet, Service→Pod (via label selectors), and Deployment→ConfigMap. The scraper uses efficient in-memory data structures, Kubernetes watch APIs for low overhead, and emits the full relationship graph every 30 seconds. Designed for minimal resource usage and continuous monitoring of large clusters without overloading the API server.

Med-Bench

NLP, Medical AI Benchmark, Computational Biology

Abstract: There exist several Large Language Models (LLMs) that can be utilized for several tasks in the medical research space, including Natural Language Inference (NLI), Question Answering (QA), and Sequence Classification. In this project, we introduce MedBench, a benchmark dataset we curated involving multiple sequence classification, question answering, and NLI tasks, all on medical data. We combined MedNLI, MedQA, Disease Symptoms, PubMedQA, and BioASQ datasets, and used text-based data augmentation to increase the number of samples. We evaluated state-of-the-art models—BioGPT, Clinical BERT, and BioBERT—on MedBench, demonstrating its utility for benchmarking LLMs in medical research.
